Family Discount for Online Quran Classes

Learn together and save on eligible family enrollments.

  • 1–2 students: Regular fees; no discount.
  • 3–4 students: 50% discount on the third student's fee.
  • 5 students: 50% discount on the fifth student's fee.
  • 6 or more students: 50% discount on the fifth student's fee and 60% discount on the sixth student's fee.
  • All other students pay their regular fees.

Example: Monthly Fees for Six Students

If each student's regular monthly fee is $60:

Students Monthly Fee
First four students $240
Fifth student (50% off) $30
Sixth student (60% off) $24
Total $294/month

In this example, the family saves $66 per month. The same discount percentages apply in other countries, using the applicable local monthly fee.

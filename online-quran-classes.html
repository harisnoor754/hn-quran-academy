Welcome to HN Quran Academy

Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuh!

HN Quran Academy provides online Quran classes for kids and adults around the world. Our aim is to help students learn the Holy Quran with correct pronunciation, proper recitation and understanding of Islamic teachings.

Whether you are a beginner learning Arabic letters or someone who wants to improve Quran recitation, our online Quran lessons are designed to support different learning levels.

We welcome students from Australia, Norway, India, the United Kingdom, the United States and other countries.

Our online learning approach allows students to study from home and arrange class timings according to their local time zones.

Online Quran Classes for Kids

HN Quran Academy offers online Quran learning for children in a supportive and friendly learning environment. Young students can begin with Noorani Qaida and gradually improve their Quran reading and pronunciation.

Parents can contact us to discuss suitable class schedules and male or female teacher availability.

Online Quran Classes for Adults

Adults who want to learn Quran reading, improve their Tajweed or revise their recitation can also join HN Quran Academy.

Beginners and learners with previous Quran knowledge can discuss their learning goals with the academy before starting classes.

Our Online Quran Courses

Explore the online Quran and Islamic learning courses available at HN Quran Academy for children and adults.

Online Noorani Qaida Classes

Learn Arabic letters, their sounds and basic pronunciation through Noorani Qaida lessons. This course is suitable for beginners and children starting their Quran learning journey.

Online Nazra Quran and Quran Reading

Improve your Quran reading skills through regular practice and teacher guidance. Students learn to read the Holy Quran with greater fluency and correct pronunciation.

Online Hifz-ul-Quran Classes

Students interested in memorizing the Holy Quran can follow a structured learning and revision routine with teacher supervision.

Learn Quran with Tajweed Online

Learn the basic and advanced rules of Tajweed to improve Quran recitation and pronunciation. Regular practice helps students develop more accurate recitation.

Online Quran Tafseer Classes

Study the meanings and explanations of Quranic verses and develop a better understanding of the teachings of the Holy Quran.

Online Islamic Studies for Kids

Children can learn basic Islamic knowledge, good manners, beliefs and everyday Islamic teachings through guided lessons.

Daily Duas, Six Kalimas and Namaz

Learn daily Masnoon Duas, the Six Kalimas and the correct method of performing Salah.
